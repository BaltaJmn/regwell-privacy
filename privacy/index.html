Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Regwell does not collect, store or share any of your data. There is no account and no server of ours.

Your highlights, notes and bookmarks

They are saved on your iPhone or iPad, in a file of the app's own. Your device's iCloud Backup may include it, as it does for other apps. If you turn on iCloud sync (Regwell Pro), they are also kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your other devices signed in to the same account get them. We cannot read them: they are stored by Apple in your account, not sent to us. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device. Deleting the app removes the file on that device.

Checking for amendments

Once a day, and when you tap "Check for amendments now", Regwell asks the eCFR (ecfr.gov, run by the Office of the Federal Register) which parts of the regulations changed, and downloads those parts. It also reads the AIM page on faa.gov to see whether a newer AIM change is out. These are plain requests for public texts: they carry no account, no identifier and nothing you wrote. Like any website, those servers see your IP address; their own privacy policies apply. When you open a link to ecfr.gov or faa.gov, your browser opens it.

Search and reading

Searching, reading and your place in each section stay on the device. Nothing you search or read is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Regwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it only asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Regwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data. After it is installed, Regwell reads every text with the network turned off.

Children

Regwell is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.
