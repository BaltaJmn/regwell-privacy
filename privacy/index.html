Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Regwell does not collect your content, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. There is no server of ours. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

Your highlights, notes and bookmarks

They are saved on your iPhone or iPad, in a file of the app's own. Your device's iCloud Backup may include it, as it does for other apps. If you turn on iCloud sync (Regwell Pro), they are also kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your other devices signed in to the same account get them. We cannot read them: they are stored by Apple in your account, not sent to us. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device. Deleting the app removes the file on that device.

Checking for amendments

Once a day, and when you tap "Check for amendments now", Regwell asks the eCFR (ecfr.gov, run by the Office of the Federal Register) which parts of the regulations changed, and downloads those parts. It also reads the AIM page on faa.gov to see whether a newer AIM change is out. These are plain requests for public texts: they carry no account, no identifier and nothing you wrote. Like any website, those servers see your IP address; their own privacy policies apply. When you open a link to ecfr.gov or faa.gov, your browser opens it.

Search and reading

Searching, reading and your place in each section stay on the device. Nothing you search or read is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Regwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Regwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Nothing else from Regwell, such as your highlights, notes, bookmarks or searches, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Regwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Regwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, described under Purchases. After it is installed, Regwell reads every text with the network turned off; the purchase record is sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Regwell is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID and the purchase record (see Purchases).
