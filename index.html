Regwell

The FAR/AIM, always current

Regwell is a reader for the Federal Aviation Regulations and the Aeronautical Information Manual, for iPhone and iPad. It has all of Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations, 49 CFR Parts 830 and 1552, the AIM with its figures and the Pilot/Controller Glossary.

Always check the official sources before you rely on a rule for a flight.

Always current

Regwell checks the eCFR (ecfr.gov) once a day and downloads only the parts that were amended. Each page says which issue of the eCFR it comes from. The AIM and the glossary come with each app update, and the app tells you when faa.gov has a newer AIM change.

Highlights that stay

Highlight in five colors, add notes and bookmark sections with your own titles. A highlight is tied to the words, not to a page: when a section is amended it stays on the same words, or on what they became. If the words are removed, it is kept and shown apart as "from an earlier text". An update never deletes one.

Search

Type a reference (91.103, 61.113(i), Part 107, AIM 5-1-1) and go straight to it, or words and phrases, with pilot shorthand such as ELT, PIC, BasicMed or TFR. See every amendment of the last two years in What changed.

Price

Reading, search, updates and bookmarks are free, with 5 highlights or notes. Regwell Pro adds unlimited highlights and notes, bookmark folders, iCloud sync and the word-by-word view of what changed: $14.99 a year or $2.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country). Highlights you made stay yours if Pro ends. Cancel anytime in your Apple account settings.

Sources

The regulations are the eCFR, published by the Office of the Federal Register and the Government Publishing Office; the eCFR is an editorial compilation, not an official legal edition of the CFR. The AIM and the Pilot/Controller Glossary are published by the FAA on faa.gov.
